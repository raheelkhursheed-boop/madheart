How it works

From the first call to the last delivery.

  1. 1

    Disaster strikes

    A flood, earthquake or fire hits a community.

  2. 2

    The disaster's protocol activates

    Each type of disaster has its own playbook: what's needed, who to alert and in what order, adjusted for the weather and the people affected.

  3. Repeats until every call is answered

    1. 3

      Calls for help are picked up automatically

      AI monitors social media, news and text messages, and sorts every request by need, place and urgency.

    2. 4

      Volunteers and resources mobilise

      Pledged volunteers and partner organizations are alerted with exactly what the protocol says this disaster needs.

    3. 5

      Help is matched to each call

      Every call is matched to the nearest help that fits. People check every match that touches someone's safety.

    4. 6

      More calls sharpen the response

      Each new call shows where needs are growing and what's running short, and feeds straight back into step 3.

  4. The loop is closed

    Help arrives, the person who asked confirms it, and the call is marked done. Nobody falls through the cracks.

Every disaster needs something different.

So every disaster gets its own protocol. Here's what it looks like in practice.

Floods

Calls for help

Families stranded on rooftops, no clean water, medicines running out.

Mobilised

  • Boats and rescue teams
  • Drinking water
  • Dry rations
  • Medical teams
  • Shelter and housing
  • Phone charging

Loop closed

Families rescued, sheltered and resupplied until the water goes down.

Earthquakes

Calls for help

People trapped under rubble, the injured, homes too unsafe to sleep in.

Mobilised

  • Search and rescue
  • Heavy equipment
  • Doctors and blood donors
  • Tents and blankets
  • Structural engineers
  • Food and water

Loop closed

Survivors found and treated, families safely sheltered through the aftershocks.

Forest fires

Calls for help

People who need a way out, a place to stay, help with pets and livestock.

Mobilised

  • Evacuation rides
  • Spare rooms and hotel stays
  • N95 masks
  • Animal shelter and trailers
  • Fuel
  • Essentials kits

Loop closed

Everyone out safely, housed nearby, and animals cared for until they can go home.

Flash floods

Calls for help

People cut off within minutes, cars swept away, relatives missing.

Mobilised

  • Swift-water rescue
  • Text alerts via telecom partners
  • High-ground shelters
  • Missing-person tracing
  • Road and route updates
  • Dry clothes and food

Loop closed

People reached in time, families reunited, everyone accounted for.
