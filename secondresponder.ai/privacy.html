Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect your email address, the country you choose, the answers you pick from our drop-down menus (how you'd like to help, or your type of organization and what it could offer), and whether you signed up as a volunteer or an organization.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about Second Responder: our launch in your country, ways to volunteer or partner, and related updates. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Waitlist sign-ups are stored with our website and form provider. As we launch in each country, we plan to keep people's data in that country wherever we can.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@secondresponder.ai
