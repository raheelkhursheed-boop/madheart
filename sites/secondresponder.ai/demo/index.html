Activity

    Anyone can report a need in their own words, by text, voice note or post. Second Responder turns it into a structured request that responders can act on, then asks a local verifier to confirm it.

    Every disaster type has an open response protocol: what's needed, in what order, adjusted for weather and the people affected. Protocols are published openly so any city, NGO or company can use them.

    0–72 hours · Rescue & relief

    • Boats and rescue teams
    • Drinking water, ready-to-eat food
    • Medicines (insulin, BP, ORS), first aid
    • Dry shelter, tarpaulins
    • Phone charging, SMS broadcast

    Days 3–14 · Stabilise

    • Cooked meals, dry rations
    • Water purification, sanitation kits
    • Medical camps, disease watch
    • Temporary housing
    • Bedding and clothing

    Weeks 2–12 · Recover

    • Clean-up crews and tools
    • House repair materials
    • Livelihood and school kits
    • Documents and claims help
    • Mental-health support

    Weather modifier · Cold nights

    Adds blankets, warm clothing and heating fuel to the 0–72 h list.

    Population modifier · Elderly & infants

    Raises priority for medicines, baby food and accessible shelter.

    Other protocols

    Earthquakes, wildfires and flash floods each have their own lists and phases.

    Crisis coordination shouldn't depend on a single private platform that can change its rules or switch off. Second Responder is built as public infrastructure.

    Open core

    Open-source matching, verification and open response protocols. Anyone can run it, audit it or improve it.

    India deployment

    Data stays in India. Local languages, local verification, state and district integrations.

    Canada deployment

    Data stays in Canada. English and French. Wildfire and flood protocols first.

    US deployment

    Data stays in the US. County and city emergency desks, community groups.

    Communities in control

    Local responders verify needs and decide what data is shared, with whom, and for how long.

    Any channel in

    Requests arrive from SMS, messaging apps, social posts and helplines, so no one needs a new app to ask for help.

    Nonprofit

    Run as a nonprofit. Public outputs stay free and open.
