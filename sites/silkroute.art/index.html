About

Why this route

For two thousand years, pashmina, lapis, paper and pigment moved along these valleys. The artists working there now are some of the most interesting in Asia, and some of the hardest to collect.

silkroute.art is a curated platform for collecting contemporary art from Kashmir, Ladakh, Central Asia, Afghanistan, Persia and the Caucasus. We start in Srinagar, where traditions like papier-mâché, sozni embroidery and walnut carving meet artists trained in Baroda, Delhi and abroad, and follow the old trade routes west.

We are artist first. We work directly with each artist. We visit studios, document every work, and agree prices together, so collectors in Toronto, London, Dubai or Delhi can buy with confidence and artists keep the majority share.

How we work

Four promises

01 · Discover

Curated at source

Our curators visit studios in each region. Artists are chosen for the strength of their practice, not their following.

02 · Authenticate

Provenance on record

Every work is photographed in the studio, signed, and issued a certificate that records the artist, date and place it was made.

03 · Pricing

Artists set the price

Prices are agreed with the artist and shown openly. You see the same price whether you are in Srinagar or San Francisco.

04 · Resell

A home for resale

Collectors who buy through us can resell through us, and the original artist receives a share of every resale.
