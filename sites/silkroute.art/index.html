What we look for

I recently started collecting contemporary art myself, including work by Tom Vattakuzhy, Misha Japanwala, Basita Shah, Aadil Malik and Monica. The more I collected, the clearer the gap became.

Most curation is not artist first. That is unfair. Artists spend so much of their lives grappling with uncertainty, yet the very practice of discovery and acquisition is loaded in favour of the gallery and the collector. silkroute.art is built the other way around.

Raheel Khursheed, founder

  • 01Artists receive the majority of the revenue from every sale made through us.
  • 02Artists are paid again whenever their work is resold.
  • 03For works that are foundational to their practice, artists can negotiate the right to exhibit the work after it has been acquired.

silkroute.art shows contemporary artists from Kashmir and the lands the old routes joined: Ladakh, Central Asia, Afghanistan, Persia and the Caucasus.

We look for a serious, sustained practice, whatever the medium or training. Some artists work inside inherited traditions such as papier-mâché, sozni, miniature painting or felt. Others have moved away from them. What matters is that the work could only have been made by this artist, from this place, now.

We choose work, not followings. We visit every artist in their studio, see every work in person before it is listed, and show nothing the artist has not approved.

We will grow slowly, one region at a time, beginning in Srinagar.
