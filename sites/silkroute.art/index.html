Manifesto

Founder’s Manifesto

RK
Raheel KhursheedFounder

I believe we are all born to engage with art.

Only a few of us are brave enough to answer that call directly. These outliers, the artists, go against the grain of societal norms of making a steady living and embrace uncertainty, financial insecurity and intense pressure, all the while, trying to develop their voice and the corresponding skills to channel that voice into a medium of their choosing. I also believe it is incumbent on the rest of us to support these outliers in as many ways and by as many means as possible. For it is only via the extension of this support can we do justice to our endeavor of a search for meaning in a seemingly meaningless context. Silkroute emerges from this urgent instinct.

My personal attempt to honor this intuition has led to the custodianship of contemporary works by Sam Samiee, Tom Vattakuzhy, Misha Japanwala, Basita Shah, Adil Malik and Monika. I refrain from using the terms ‘acquisition’ and/or ‘collection’ because I feel they commodify what is at its core an anti-capitalist endeavor. After all, none of the artists I have worked with have produced these works with the expectation of a financial reward. They would continue to make art even if no one ‘acquired’ it from them.

Ironically, yet predictably, over the years, an infrastructure of financialisation has wrapped itself around the production and consumption of art. This infrastructure forces the artists to participate in the exact same process they shunned & cuts off access to their work from those who might honour it the best. Most galleries charge a 50 percent commission on the sale of an art work and offer no proceeds from the resale of their works, however astronomical the increase in the value of the work. The process of curation, as it stands, works neither for the artists nor for the custodians of the work.

silkroute.art is built the other way around. Born out of multiple conversations with artist friends at various stages of their journeys, our foundational ethic is artist first. Our terms address long-standing artists’ desires for equity, ownership and an access to an audience that honors the years of blood and sweat that go into developing an artistic practice. We are also making a commitment to the security and growth of artistic talent at various stages of development. To that end, silkroute.art will allocate part of its proceeds from the sale of art works towards building a residency program and a stipend program, the details of which are forthcoming.

I invite you to join us and help us build a more equitable & ethical home for artists and art at silkroute.art.

The practice

Serious and sustained, whatever the medium

Some artists work inside inherited traditions such as papier-mâché, sozni, miniature painting or felt. Others have moved away from them. What matters is that the work could only have been made by this artist, from this place, now.

The work

We choose work, not followings

We work with the artist on curation and we display nothing the artist has not approved.

The place

One region at a time

We begin in Srinagar and gradually curate artists either from other parts of the Silk Route or with a connection to the route.

Artists

Built for the artist

  1. The majority share

    Artists receive the majority of the revenue from every sale made through us.

  2. Paid again on resale

    Artists are paid again whenever their work is resold.

  3. The right to exhibit

    For works that are foundational to their practice, artists can negotiate the right to exhibit the work after it finds its custodian.

Built with artists —
for artists and art lovers.

All works verified for medium, size, date & provenance.

Many people would love to live with original art but don’t know where to start. Not for lack of taste, but because nobody shows them how. Galleries often feel closed. Prices are hidden behind "on request" and it is hard to assess what a fair price is, or whether you are even allowed to ask. We want to make this process such that your first custodianship feels as considered as your tenth, without needing to know anyone.

Custodians

For first-time custodians

We are reimagining the idea of a collector. Instead, we want you to think of yourself as a custodian. This encourages an ongoing relationship, both with the artist and the art work that is in your care.

  1. Start with a conversation, not a catalogue

    Tell us what you keep coming back to, where the work will live and what you want to spend. We send back a short curated list of artists and works to look at.

  2. Get to know the artist

    Every work comes with the artist's own account of how and why it was made. Where the artist is willing, you can speak with them or visit the studio before you decide.

  3. Every price in the open

    Prices are set by the artist and shown on every work. No "price on request", and no different price for people we know.
