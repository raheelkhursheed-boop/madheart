For first-time collectors

Many people would love to live with original art but have never bought a piece. Not for lack of taste, but because nobody shows them where to start.

Your first work, in five steps
  1. Start with a conversation
  2. Meet the artist
  3. Every price in the open
  4. Begin small, if you like
  5. Know what you are buying
01 · A conversation, not a catalogue

Start with a conversation, not a catalogue

Tell us what you keep coming back to, where the work will live and what you want to spend. We send back a short list of artists and works to look at, not a hundred.

Your short listThree artists, chosen for the room you described.
02 · The person behind the work

Meet the artist

Every work comes with the artist's own account of how and why it was made. Where the artist is willing, you can speak with them or visit the studio before you decide.

In the artist's wordsHow and why the work was made, written by the artist.
03 · No "price on request"

Every price in the open

Prices are set by the artist and shown on every work. No "price on request", and no different price for people we know.

04 · Smaller works and works on paper

A first work does not have to be a big one

We show smaller works and works on paper alongside major pieces, so you can begin with something you love at a price you are comfortable with.

05 · Seen in person, made by hand

Know exactly what you are buying

We have seen every work in person. Each one comes with its medium, size, date, the story of where it came from and how to care for it, and the guarantee that it was made by human hands.

With every workMedium, size and date. Where it came from. How to care for it. Made by human hands.

A few honest rules for a first purchase

  1. i.Buy the work you keep looking at, not the one you think you should like.
  2. ii.Decide on a budget before you fall in love, and tell us. It helps us, not just you.
  3. iii.Ask the artist anything. Most are glad to be asked.
  4. iv.Your first work starts a relationship with an artist. When you buy here, most of the money goes to them, and they are paid again if the work is ever resold.

A note from the founder. I recently started collecting contemporary art myself, including work by Tom Vattakuzhy, Misha Japanwala, Basita Shah, Aadil Malik and Monica. The more I collected, the clearer the gap became.

Most curation is not artist first. That is unfair. Artists spend so much of their lives grappling with uncertainty, yet the very practice of discovery and acquisition is loaded in favour of the gallery and the collector. silkroute.art is built the other way around.
