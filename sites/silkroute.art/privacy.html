Privacy

silkroute.art collects only what you type into our forms: your email address and, if you give them, your name, city, country, portfolio link and a note about your work.

How we use it

Artist applications are read by our curators to decide whether to arrange a studio visit. Collector sign-ups receive occasional updates about new artists and works, at most once a month.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or share your details, and we don’t use advertising trackers on this site.

Removing your details

Reply to any email from us asking to be removed and we will delete your details.
